Frequently Asked Questions

Is VibeScore AI actually powered by AI? +

Yes. Your idea is sent to this site's own server, which forwards it to a language model hosted on OpenRouter, then displays the answer inside enough gradients to make the intelligence feel at least 60% more advanced.

Will this guarantee my startup succeeds? +

Absolutely not. It is a brainstorming tool, not market validation, investment advice, or a substitute for speaking to real customers.

What do the four scores actually mean? +

Vibe Score and Market Need are better when high. Slop Risk is better when low — a high Slop Risk means your idea looks like a generic AI wrapper. Build Difficulty is neither good nor bad; it just tells you what you are signing up for. There is a full explainer under your scores once you run an analysis.

Can I argue with the results? +

Yes. Every analysis comes with a follow-up box that lets you ask the model up to eight questions about what it said — what to fix first, what to cut, or why it was so confident about your Slop Risk. It keeps the whole analysis and the conversation so far in front of it, so you can push back and it will remember. The thread is not saved anywhere: reload the page or run a new analysis and it starts over.

Why are there so many emojis? +

Because every generic AI landing page needs visual proof that the future is exciting, friendly, magical, disruptive, and vaguely purple. ✨🚀🧠

Is my idea private? +

No. Your idea is sent to this site's server, which forwards it to OpenRouter and on to a model provider. That means this site's operator and those third parties can see whatever you submit. Nothing is encrypted end-to-end and nothing here is confidential. Do not enter private information, client data, or anything you would not paste into a stranger's website.
